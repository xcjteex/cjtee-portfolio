What I Help With

The problems that usually bring someone to a bookkeeper

"My books are behind and I don't trust the numbers."
Cleanup and catch-up work: resolving unreconciled transactions, fixing miscategorization, finding missing or duplicate entries, and bringing records current so you can rely on them again.
"I don't know where I actually stand."
Month-end close, monthly and financial reporting, and cash flow visibility, so you can see your position without digging for it.
"Billing, vendors, and approvals live in my head."
Accounts payable and receivable, invoicing and billing, and vendor and procurement workflow, handled on a schedule instead of an as-remembered basis.
"Things are getting missed because nobody owns the process."
Documentation, follow-ups, approvals, vendor coordination, recurring administrative tasks, and process tracking need a clear owner and a reliable system.
